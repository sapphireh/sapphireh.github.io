Learning How to Align: Elastic Domain Adaptation for Improving Reasoning Robustness of Large Language Models

Yujie Hou, Mei Wang, Yaoyao Zhong, Guozhang Li, Qiannan Zhu, and Hua Huang

Adaptive layer selection and perturbation-specific alignment for more robust reasoning under question variations.

Paper ↗ PDF ↗ Code ↗
Research summary

EDA treats reasoning under question perturbations as an unsupervised domain adaptation problem. Adaptive Layer Selection identifies layers for alignment, while the Elastic Shift Module adjusts representations for different perturbation types. The study evaluates six language models across 15 perturbation types.

ACL 2026Long paper

SMART: Evaluating LLMs’ Mathematical Reasoning via a Human Cognitive Process-Inspired Benchmark

Yujie Hou, Mei Wang, Yaoyao Zhong, Ting Zhang, Xuetao Ma, and Hua Huang

A process-oriented benchmark that separates understanding, reasoning, computation, and reflection.

Paper ↗ PDF ↗ Hugging Face ↗
Research summary

Inspired by Pólya’s problem-solving theory, SMART evaluates semantic understanding, mathematical reasoning, arithmetic computation, and reflection and refinement separately. Evaluations of 22 language models reveal uneven capabilities across these dimensions. The All-Pass Score summarizes performance across the full problem-solving process.

ACM Multimedia 2025Outstanding Paper

Robust Multi-view Clustering via Pseudo Label Guided Universum Learning

Zhenxi Wang, Zongyao Yin, Yujie Hou, and Xianchuan Yu

Pseudo-label-guided universum learning to reduce false negatives and improve multi-view clustering.

Paper ↗ Code ↗ Award ↗
Research summary

PAUSE first learns pseudo labels through dual contrastive learning, then uses them to mix samples with out-of-class centroids. These universum samples help separate clusters while preserving cohesion within each class. The paper received Outstanding Paper recognition in the Content Theme at ACM Multimedia 2025.

ACCV 2022Computer vision

Multi-Branch Network with Ensemble Learning for Text Removal in the Wild

Yujie Hou, Jiwei Chen, and Zengfu Wang

Complementary text-removal branches and ensemble fusion to erase scene text while preserving background details.

Paper ↗ PDF ↗
Research summary

MBE combines a main branch for high-resolution background texture with two sub-branches for multi-scale semantic features. Feature-level and image-level fusion integrate their complementary outputs, while patch attention helps localize text regions.
