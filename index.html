Learning How to Align: Elastic Domain Adaptation for Improving Reasoning Robustness of Large Language Models

Yujie Hou, Mei Wang, Yaoyao Zhong, Guozhang Li, Qiannan Zhu, and Hua Huang

Adaptive layer selection and perturbation-specific alignment for more robust reasoning under question variations.

Paper ↗ PDF ↗ Code ↗
Research summary

EDA treats reasoning under question perturbations as an unsupervised domain adaptation problem. Adaptive Layer Selection identifies layers for alignment, while the Elastic Shift Module adjusts representations for different perturbation types. The study evaluates six language models across 15 perturbation types.

SMART: Evaluating LLMs’ Mathematical Reasoning via a Human Cognitive Process-Inspired Benchmark

Yujie Hou, Mei Wang, Yaoyao Zhong, Ting Zhang, Xuetao Ma, and Hua Huang

A process-oriented benchmark that separates understanding, reasoning, computation, and reflection.

Paper ↗ PDF ↗ Repository ↗
Research summary

Inspired by Pólya’s problem-solving theory, SMART evaluates semantic understanding, mathematical reasoning, arithmetic computation, and reflection and refinement separately. Evaluations of 22 language models reveal uneven capabilities across these dimensions. The All-Pass Score summarizes performance across the full problem-solving process.
